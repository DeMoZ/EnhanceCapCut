# EnhanceCapCut — presentation and promo scripts

Text for a presentation of the app and for promo videos: slides with what to show on screen and what the narrator
says, then ready cuts of 15, 30 and 60 seconds. The in-app guide (Help ▸ EnhanceCapCut Help, the "How to Use" link
in the export window) is the same story told as instructions: `Sources/EnhanceCapCut/GuideView.swift`,
`windows/src/EnhanceCapCut.App/GuideWindow.cs`.

Keep the claims in line with the app (see "Claims" at the end): when a feature changes, update this file too.

## The idea in one line

**Your CapCut project, exported your way — and dubbed into any language, without leaving CapCut.**

Shorter variants for titles and thumbnails:

- Export CapCut projects your way.
- CapCut + your export settings + AI dubbing.
- One click from CapCut to 4K, HEVC or ProRes.
- Speak every language — in your own voice.

## Who it's for

- Creators who edit in CapCut and want more control over the file they publish: resolution, codec, bitrate,
  loudness.
- Travel, food and vlog channels that want subtitles and voice-over in several languages without paying per minute.
- Anyone who wants their video to stay on their computer: everything runs offline.

## Presentation

Each slide: the title, the text on screen, what the video shows, and the narration.

### 1. Title

- **On screen:** EnhanceCapCut. *Export your CapCut projects your way.*
- **Show:** the app icon over a CapCut timeline.
- **Narration:** "You edit in CapCut. EnhanceCapCut takes it from there."

### 2. The problem

- **On screen:** Fixed export presets. Subtitles in one language. Dubbing that costs money.
- **Show:** CapCut's export dialog; a comment section in other languages.
- **Narration:** "CapCut is a great editor. But the export gives you a few presets, the captions speak one language,
  and dubbing your video for other countries usually means an online service and a monthly bill."

### 3. The solution

- **On screen:** Your settings. Your languages. Your computer.
- **Show:** the export window opening over CapCut.
- **Narration:** "EnhanceCapCut is a small app that works next to CapCut. It reads the project you're editing,
  renders it with its own engine, and adds subtitles and voice-over right into the project."

### 4. One click from CapCut

- **On screen:** CapCut ▸ Services ▸ Export with EnhanceCapCut. Or your hotkey.
- **Show (Mac):** the Services menu in CapCut, then the export window with the project already selected.
  **(Windows):** clicking the pinned icon in the taskbar.
- **Narration:** "No importing, no files to look for. Press your hotkey in CapCut, and the project you're editing is
  already there."

### 5. Export your way

- **On screen:** Up to 4K · 24–60 fps · H.264 · HEVC 10-bit · ProRes · quality or bitrate.
- **Show:** scrolling through the Video section: aspect ratio, resolution, codec, the quality slider.
- **Narration:** "Pick the resolution, the frame rate and the codec. Constant quality or an exact bitrate. Switch the
  aspect ratio, and the canvas is laid out again, like in CapCut."

### 6. Sound that's ready to publish

- **On screen:** Loudness normalization · −14 LUFS
- **Show:** the Normalize loudness switch and the Target field.
- **Narration:** "Turn on loudness normalization, and every video comes out at the level YouTube, Instagram and
  TikTok expect. No more one video too quiet and the next one too loud."

### 7. Know before you export

- **On screen:** What isn't rendered yet. Missing media.
- **Show:** the "Not rendered yet" section of a real project.
- **Narration:** "Before you export, the window tells you which CapCut features in your project it doesn't reproduce
  yet, and which media files are missing. No surprises at the end."

### 8. Subtitles in every language

- **On screen:** Transcribe · translate · add to the project.
- **Show:** the Subtitles & Voice-over window: spoken language, Translate to, Shown on screen; then CapCut with the new
  caption tracks.
- **Narration:** "Choose the spoken language and the languages to translate into. EnhanceCapCut transcribes the speech,
  translates it, and adds each language to your CapCut project as its own caption track. They look like your
  captions, and you edit them in CapCut like any other."

### 9. Voice-over

- **On screen:** A voice for every language.
- **Show:** picking voices, the ▶ preview, the Original audio choices; then the new timeline in CapCut playing the
  dub.
- **Narration:** "Want them to hear it, not read it? Pick a voice for each language. Your original sound can be kept,
  lowered, muted, or the voice alone removed so the music and the ambience stay. The dub goes to a new timeline, and
  the original stays untouched."

### 10. Your own voice, in any language

- **On screen:** Clone the original voice. With consent.
- **Show:** the Clone the original voice switch, Expressiveness, the consent question; then before/after of one line.
- **Narration:** "Or let the video speak in the speaker's own voice, with the same intonation and emotion, in twenty
  languages. Use it for your own voice, or for people who agreed. The app asks every time."

### 11. Private and offline

- **On screen:** Runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Show:** the app working with Wi-Fi off.
- **Narration:** "Speech recognition, translation and voices run on your Mac or PC. Your video never leaves it. The
  models are downloaded once, and that's it."

### 12. Safe for your projects

- **On screen:** Backup before every change.
- **Show:** the Show Backup button after a run.
- **Narration:** "Before it changes a project, EnhanceCapCut backs it up. Run it again, and it replaces what it added
  before, while your own captions stay."

### 13. Get it

- **On screen:** macOS 15+ · Windows 10/11 · github.com/DeMoZ/EnhanceCapCut
- **Show:** the Releases page; the installer.
- **Narration:** "EnhanceCapCut is free, for Mac and Windows. Download it from GitHub, and if it saves you time,
  support it with the Donate button."

## Claims

What the texts above say and where it holds, so the promo stays truthful:

- **4K, frame rates, codecs:** resolution up to 2160p (short side); 24, 25, 30, 60 fps or the project's. H.264, HEVC
  8- and 10-bit on both; ProRes 422 / 422 HQ only on the Mac. Show ProRes only in Mac footage.
- **Loudness:** on the Mac it needs ffmpeg installed; built in on Windows.
- **Not everything CapCut does is rendered:** effects, filters, transitions, stickers and text animations aren't yet.
  Pick demo projects that look the same in our export, and don't claim "pixel-perfect" or "everything CapCut does".
- **Offline:** speech, translation and voices run locally; models are downloaded once. Updates and the donation list
  use the internet. On the Mac, subtitles need macOS 26 (Apple Translation).
- **Twenty languages** is voice cloning (Chatterbox Multilingual); other languages are spoken by system voices.
  Translation covers 24 languages.
- **"Replace voice"** (keep music and ambience, drop the speech) is Mac only; Windows offers "Remove vocal" through
  CapCut.
- **Free:** the app is free to download from GitHub Releases; donations are voluntary. Don't call it open source:
  the repo has no licence.
- **Voice cloning consent:** always mention it when cloning is shown.
- Don't use CapCut's logo or suggest an official connection: EnhanceCapCut is an independent app that works with
  CapCut projects.
