## Voice-over

**macOS:** the **Subtitles & Voice-over** window now does voice-over too. Translated lines are spoken by
on-device Apple voices (choose female or male, a voice per language, ▶ to preview) and added to the CapCut
project as audio tracks, one per language, with a clip per line so lines can be moved or trimmed in CapCut.
One language is heard, the others are added muted. Each line is sped up just enough to fit before the next.
The original sound can be kept, lowered by 15 dB or muted while the voice speaks (volume keyframes on its
clips, adjustable in CapCut). Siri voices are offered too when Xcode is installed. Better voices: download
Enhanced or Premium voices in System Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Read & Speak.

Running again replaces the voice-over and ducking added before; speech recognition ignores earlier voice-over.

**Command line (macOS):** `ecc subtitles … --voice en,th --hear en --original keep|lower|mute --no-captions`,
and `ecc voices <language>` lists the installed voices.

**Rendering (both platforms):** volume keyframes on clips are now played in exports, also inside compound clips.

## Downloads

- `EnhanceCapCut-0.4.0.pkg` — macOS app (Apple Silicon and Intel), installs into /Applications.
- `EnhanceCapCut-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip` — Windows command-line tool `ecc.exe` (`list`, `info`, `render`,
  `subtitles`); unzip anywhere, no .NET install needed. Voice-over is Mac only for now.

Both builds are unsigned: on macOS, allow the installer in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security; on Windows,
SmartScreen may ask to confirm.
