## Fuller subtitles; Whisper on the Mac

**Subtitles on the Mac** catch many more words. Apple's dictation (the recogniser for Russian and many other
languages) dropped about half of the speech in a whole noisy timeline. It now gets the audio in short pieces cut
at pauses, and on a street-market vlog it recognised 30 words instead of 18. Word timing stays close to CapCut's
own captions (within about 0.1 s).

**New: choose the speech recognition.** The Subtitles & Voice-over window has a **Speech recognition** row:
Apple (built into macOS) or **Whisper large-v3-turbo**, the same recogniser as the Windows version. Whisper catches
more in noisy dialogue: measured against CapCut's own captions on four vlogs, it missed half as many words as Apple
on the hardest one. Its model (574 MB) is downloaded once, from the window or on the first run. On the command
line: `ecc subtitles … --engine whisper`.

**Windows subtitles** covered only the first ~30 seconds of a video. They now cover all of it, with word timing
corrected by the 0.12 s that Whisper's alignment was late.

**Both:** Whisper no longer adds "*смех*", "[музыка]" or "♪" over noise where nobody speaks.

**Audio fix:** when the sound of a compound clip was extracted to its own audio track, the compound still played
its own sound too, so the voice was doubled in exports and in speech recognition. Projects like that are worth
exporting again.

## Downloads

- `EnhanceCapCut-0.6.0.pkg` — macOS app (Apple Silicon and Intel), installs into /Applications.
- `EnhanceCapCut-0.6.0-windows-x64.zip` — Windows app `EnhanceCapCut.exe` and command-line tool `ecc.exe`; unzip
  anywhere, no .NET or Windows App SDK install needed.

Both builds are unsigned: on macOS, allow the installer in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security; on Windows,
SmartScreen may ask to confirm.
